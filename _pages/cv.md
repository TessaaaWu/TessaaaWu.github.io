<h2>Research Interests</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Artificial intelligence in geophysics:</strong> machine learning, Bayesian inference, and uncertainty quantification for large-scale electromagnetic, seismic, gravity, and magnetic data integration.</li>
<li><strong>Sustainable resources and energy systems:</strong> exploration and monitoring of geothermal, critical mineral, and natural hydrogen and geological carbon storage systems.</li>
<li><strong>Hydrogeophysics and near-surface processes:</strong> groundwater flow, salinity and contamination, CO<sub>2</sub>-brine interactions, time-lapse monitoring, and quantitative characterization of near-surface heterogeneity.</li>
<li><strong>Crustal and lithospheric dynamics:</strong> multi-physics imaging of crust and upper-mantle structure, melt and fluid distribution, and fluid-rock interactions that link deep Earth dynamics to resource distribution and geothermal activity.</li>
</ul>
